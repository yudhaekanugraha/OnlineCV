I study organisational resilience in small tourism businesses facing recurrent crises. My research examines the role of crisis leadership and dynamic capabilities in helping owner-managers respond to disruption and adapt over time.

Research

Organisational resilience

I examine the responses of small tourism businesses to recurrent disruption, including the 2024–2025 Mount Lewotobi eruptions affecting Labuan Bajo, Indonesia.

Dynamic capabilities

I investigate the practices that help small businesses sense change, learn from disruption, integrate knowledge and coordinate responses.

Crisis leadership

I study what owner-managers pay attention to during crises and the role of that attention in shaping strategic choices and the competencies they draw on.
